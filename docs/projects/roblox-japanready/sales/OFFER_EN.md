# Roblox Japan Launch Sprint

## Improve the Japanese player experience before investing in a full localization team

A focused seven-day audit and launch plan for Roblox studios that already have a live Experience but need clearer Japanese localization, mobile UI, store copy, and measurable Japan-market experiments.

## Who this is for

This sprint is designed for small and mid-sized Roblox studios that:

- already operate a live, actively maintained Experience;
- serve players mainly outside Japan;
- use no Japanese localization, incomplete localization, or machine translation without in-context QA;
- want evidence before hiring a dedicated Japan team or funding a larger localization project.

## What we review

### 1. Japanese localization QA

- missing or inconsistent player-facing strings;
- unnatural wording caused by literal or machine translation;
- terminology consistency across onboarding, gameplay, rewards, and purchases;
- text that is embedded in images, product names, badges, passes, or other areas not fully covered by automatic translation.

### 2. Mobile UI and readability

- text truncation, wrapping, small type, and button-label fit;
- hierarchy and clarity on common mobile screen sizes;
- Japanese text expansion and high-risk interface moments.

### 3. Store and monetization copy

- Experience title and description;
- game-pass, product, subscription, and offer wording;
- clarity, trust, and age-appropriate purchase communication;
- misleading or high-pressure wording risks.

### 4. Player journey and measurement

- onboarding and early-session friction hypotheses;
- Japan-specific retention and monetization questions;
- a practical measurement plan using the data you already have;
- a prioritized 30-day experiment backlog.

## Deliverables

Within seven days you receive:

1. one-page executive summary;
2. Japanese player-journey audit;
3. localization QA matrix with recommended copy;
4. mobile UI risk list;
5. store and monetization copy recommendations;
6. prioritized 30-day experiment plan;
7. KPI measurement sheet;
8. open questions, data gaps, and implementation notes;
9. 30-minute review call or written walkthrough.

## Pilot scope and price

**Design-partner pilot: USD 500–650** for the first three suitable studios.

This is an introductory validation price, not a claim about an industry-wide market rate. Final scope and price are confirmed after reviewing the Experience and available access/data.

## What is not included

Unless separately agreed, the sprint does not include:

- guaranteed increases in revenue, retention, ranking, or discovery;
- direct production deployment or access to sensitive systems;
- legal certification or regulatory advice;
- voice acting, custom 3D assets, advertising spend, or community management;
- a complete rewrite of the Experience;
- collection or storage of identity, payment, tax, or banking information.

## What we need from you

For a public-information audit, only the Experience and creator/studio URLs are required.

For a deeper paid engagement, useful inputs include:

- current supported languages;
- target player age and region;
- recent release notes;
- top game passes/products/subscriptions;
- country-level DAU, D1/D7 retention, and revenue reports, if you choose to share them;
- known player complaints or support themes.

We can begin without an API key. If analytics API access is later approved, it must be least-privilege, limited to the relevant Universe, stored outside the repository, and revoked or rotated after the engagement.

**Days 5–6:** experiment design and measurement plan
